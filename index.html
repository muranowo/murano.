About

Who's building this

I'm Nikhil — a product designer and developer who likes interfaces with a bit of logic underneath them.

Most of my work sits between design and engineering: I sketch the interaction, then build it myself, which usually means the two stay honest with each other. The particles above aren't decoration for its own sake — they're the same golden-angle spiral you'd find in a sunflower head, a Lissajous curve you'd see on an oscilloscope, and a sine field, all driven by the same small set of physics rules. I like that kind of thing: a simple equation that produces something worth looking at.

Based in Kathmandu, working with teams anywhere.

Approach

How a project runs

01

Discover

Understand the actual problem, the constraints, and who the interface is really for, before touching a design tool.

02

Design

Explore structure and flow first, visual language second — wireframes and prototypes people can actually click through.

03

Build

Ship the real thing, not a static comp. Building it myself keeps the design honest about what's actually possible.

04

Refine

Watch how it's actually used, then tighten the rough edges. The first version is a draft, not the destination.
